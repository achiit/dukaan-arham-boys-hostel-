About the hostel

We kept it small on purpose

I started Arham Boys Hostel in 2026 after seeing my younger cousin struggle to find a decent place in Jhang. Everywhere was crowded, the food was poor and the WiFi never worked when he needed to study.

So I built the hostel I wished he had: only 6 rooms, so I know every boy by name, and a kitchen that cooks the same food we eat at home. I am here on Chiniot Road every day, and you can call me directly.

  • Only 6 rooms, so the hostel never feels crowded
  • Fresh homemade breakfast and dinner, daily
  • Two separate 20 Mbps WiFi connections
  • Quiet study environment, study table and chairs in rooms
  • Owner is on site and reachable on WhatsApp
